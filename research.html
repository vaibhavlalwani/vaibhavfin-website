Peer-reviewed publications

Factor timing with transaction costs
Vaibhav Lalwani and Ashok Gunturu
Borsa Istanbul Review · 100913 · 2026
DOI
Times factor exposures using 50 net-of-cost smart-beta factors, driven by their past returns and covariances. The dynamic strategy beats a static 1/N benchmark on risk-adjusted returns even after 2000's fading premiums, with low turnover and results robust to specification choices.
Climate news betas and risk premia
Vaibhav Lalwani
Energy Economics · 157, 109289 · 2026
DOI
Tests whether climate-news betas are priced in US and Chinese stocks, and shows the answer swings widely with innocuous modelling choices. After correcting for multiple testing, no climate-risk premium survives.
Empirical Asset Pricing via Machine Learning: The Role of Research Design Choices
Vaibhav Lalwani, Vedprakash Vasantrao Meshram and Varun Jindal
European Financial Management · 32(3), 816–833 · 2025
DOIPDF
Shows that research-design choices move machine-learning portfolio returns so much that their nonstandard errors are up to five times the usual standard errors. Even so, ML still earns net-of-cost returns for about a third of portfolios.
Does economic complexity tame the stock markets? International evidence
Vaibhav Lalwani and Kirti Saxena
Applied Economics · 1–17 · 2025
DOI
Shows that countries producing more diverse and unique goods tend to have calmer stock markets. The effect is concentrated in emerging markets and only kicks in past a complexity threshold.
Finfluencer recommendations
Vaibhav Lalwani
Economics Letters · 255, 112511 · 2025
DOI
Studies stock calls from Indian financial YouTubers. Finfluencers favour past winners with heavy trading, rich valuations, and high intraday activity, and the number of their recommendations predicts future returns.
Mean-variance optimization and the cross-section of stock returns
Vaibhav Lalwani
Global Finance Journal · 66, 101130 · 2025
DOI
Builds an asset-pricing factor from Sharpe-ratio-maximising mean-variance weights, and finds it is priced in the cross-section of stocks and bonds, out of sample and across countries.
Forecasting equity returns with oil prices: addressing model choice uncertainty
Vaibhav Lalwani and Udayan Sharma
Applied Economics · 57(5), 566–582 · 2024
DOI
Adapts extreme bounds analysis to out-of-sample forecasting, and finds oil prices carry incremental information for aggregate and sectoral stock returns once combined with other oil variables.
Inflation hedging via tracking portfolios in the BRICS markets
Vedprakash Vasantrao Meshram and Vaibhav Lalwani
Cogent Economics & Finance · 12(1) · 2024
DOI
Builds portfolios that track unexpected CPI inflation in the BRICS markets. Statistical tracking beats the benchmarks, especially when inflation uncertainty is low.
Climate risks, corporate bonds, and economic uncertainty
Vaibhav Lalwani
Economics Letters · 244, 111984 · 2024
DOI
Shows corporate bonds that move with global-warming news earn lower returns. The gap shows up mainly in periods of high economic policy uncertainty, when investors pay up for climate-resilient bonds.
Incorporating green assets in equity portfolios
Vaibhav Lalwani
Finance research letters · 59, 104815 · 2023
DOI
Adds green-asset ETFs to mean-variance portfolios. Mixing them with broad market indices lifts returns and Sharpe ratios, survives transaction costs, and long-horizon investors are more likely to beat the benchmark.
